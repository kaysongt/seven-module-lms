Who we are

A house that sends people out stronger than they came in.

KingsWord Chicago is the apostolic headquarters of a global ministry with churches across Africa, Europe and North America. We are here to train, equip and release God's children into their divine purpose — teaching victory in life through the Word and the ministry of the Holy Spirit.

Whether it is your first Sunday or your fifteenth year, there is a place for you here, and a next step waiting when you are ready to take it.

Find your place

Our mission

To proclaim Jesus, the Anointed One, and present Him clearly to a dying world.

That mission shapes everything here — what gets taught on a Sunday, what we train people for, and who we send out.

Believers Training

Seven modules. One settled believer.

Our discipleship track takes you through covenant, identity, Scripture, the Holy Spirit, prayer, purpose and stewardship — seventy-seven lessons with a checkpoint at the end of every module. Work at your own pace, online.

What Sunday actually looks like.
